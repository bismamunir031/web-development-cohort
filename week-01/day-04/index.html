About IEEE Student Society

The IEEE (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers) Student Society at our university is a platform for students to explore technology, build practical skills, and connect with like-minded peers.

Members get the chance to attend workshops, technical talks, and competitions that support both academic learning and real-world engineering experience.

Our Activities

Below is an unordered list of regular society activities.

  • Technical workshops on programming and electronics
  • Guest speaker sessions with industry professionals
  • Robotics and circuit-building competitions
  • Peer study groups for engineering courses
  • Community service and outreach projects
